Grow With Us, Every Month

A steady monthly gift lets NCS plan ahead — for teachers, for families who need help affording tuition, and for the everyday materials that make a Christ-centered classroom come alive.

Why Give

Every family who walks through our doors is trusting us with something sacred — their child's heart, mind, and faith. Your gift is what makes that possible for the family who couldn't otherwise afford it.

No child is turned away for cost

Every year, families who love what NCS stands for still can't cover full tuition. Monthly gifts are what let us say yes to them anyway.

Small classes stay small

Individual attention isn't free — it's a deliberate choice that only holds together when giving fills the gap tuition alone doesn't cover.

Faith stays at the center

Chapel, prayer, and character formation aren't add-ons here. They're the reason NCS exists — and the reason so many families choose us.
